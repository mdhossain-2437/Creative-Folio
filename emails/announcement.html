◇  Works / 03  ·  Released 06.27  ·  The Compiled Thought

{{first_name}}, the second edition is finished. Aura Void v2 is an ambient WebGL world you don’t play so much as inhabit — a slow weather system for the browser.

The entire scene breathes off a single GLSL noise field; nothing is baked, nothing is a texture. The cursor became a physics body this time — mass, drag, and a little reluctance — so the void answers your hand with weight. Typography, motion, and the shader were tuned together until they read as one material.
