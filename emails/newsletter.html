Flexible page transitions, borrowed from Patrick Heng.

Journal · 09 June 2027 · Read (11 min)

Most page transitions fight the browser. They freeze scroll, lock the DOM, and hope nobody resizes mid-animation. Patrick Heng’s approach inverts the contract—the outgoing view is photographed, the incoming view is built underneath it, and a single curtain mediates between them. I rebuilt the pattern in Next.js with the View Transitions API as a progressive layer, and kept a GSAP fallback for the browsers still catching up.

The surprise wasn’t the motion. It was the bookkeeping—scroll restoration, focus return, and the half-second where two pages legally coexist. This dispatch is the field notes.
